# RESELLER AND DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

**Page 1 — Term.** Six (6) years from 1 January 2024, expiring 31 December 2029.

**Page 4 — Section 4.1 Appointment.** Supplier appoints Reseller as its **sole and exclusive**
distributor of the Harborline Platform in the Territory.

**Page 4 — Section 4.2 Territory.** "Territory" means the member states of the European Union,
the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Norway.

**Page 5 — Section 4.3 Exclusivity Covenant.** During the Term, **Supplier shall not, whether
directly or through any affiliate, agent, or other reseller, market, license, sell, or deliver the
Platform to any customer whose principal place of business is within the Territory**, nor solicit
any such customer. Supplier shall refer all inbound enquiries originating in the Territory to
Reseller within five (5) Business Days.

**Page 6 — Section 4.4 Minimum Commitment.** Reseller shall procure minimum annual licence
purchases as set out at Schedule 1. Failure to meet the minimum in any two consecutive years
entitles Supplier to convert the appointment to non-exclusive **on twelve (12) months' notice**.

**Page 7 — Section 4.5.** Reseller met the minimum commitment in FY2024 and FY2025.

**Page 12 — Section 8.2 Early Termination.** Supplier may terminate for convenience on
**twenty-four (24) months' written notice**, subject to payment of a termination sum equal to two
(2) times the trailing twelve month gross margin earned by Reseller.

**Page 30 — Section 22.1 Change of Control.** Assignable to a successor in interest on notice.
Exclusivity survives any assignment and binds any successor in interest.
