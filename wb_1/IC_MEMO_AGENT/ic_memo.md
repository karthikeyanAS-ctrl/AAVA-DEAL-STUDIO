## 2. Investment Thesis

The investment case for Harborline rests on a recurring-revenue SaaS platform with verified FY2025 revenue of **$42.7M** [E-027], a two-year CAGR of **11.3%** (versus management's claimed 14.0%) [E-028], closing ARR of **$38.7M** [E-039 via SQL-001], verified GRR of **97.7%** [E-004], and a top-10 customer average tenure of **6.2 years** [E-030]. These metrics provide a credible recurring-revenue foundation. However, diligence has materially challenged the earnings quality, retention narrative, and growth thesis presented in the CIM.

**What diligence supports:** Verified revenue growth, GRR broadly in line with management's claim, long-tenured top-10 customer relationships, and transaction costs and executive recruitment fees as legitimate one-time EBITDA adjustments.

**What diligence challenges:** (i) Management's adjusted EBITDA of $12.4M is reduced to $5.46M on a verified basis — a 56% reduction — due to five rejected adjustments totalling $6.94M [E-010, E-011]; (ii) NRR of 105.0% is 13 percentage points below the claimed 118% [E-004]; (iii) logo churn of 8.89% is nearly double the claimed sub-5% [E-004]; (iv) the single largest customer (Northwind, 27.3% of closing ARR) has a contractual change-of-control consent and termination right that directly contradicts the CIM's assignability representation [E-023, E-024]; (v) the primary growth vector — direct European expansion worth $6M ARR by FY2028 — is contractually blocked by the Atlas exclusivity covenant through 2029 [E-020, E-021, E-084]; (vi) revenue CAGR is 11.3% versus the claimed 14.0% [E-028]; and (vii) independently restated recurring revenue is 87.4% versus management's tagged 98.3% [E-007].

**What remains unresolved:** The EBITDA bridge discrepancy between the verified bridge ($5.46M) and the Reviewer-referenced figure ($7.25M); the status of the Vantage MFN credit obligation; whether the Atlas exclusivity covenant has been or can be waived; monthly availability data for Vantage to determine whether the Section 12.2 termination right is currently exercisable; and the collectability of $6.2M in unpaid FY2025 invoices [E-016].
