# Board of Directors — Minutes (Extract), 22 October 2025

**Item 4 — Customer Matters.**
The CRO reported continued service instability at **Vantage Grocers**, with Severity 1 incident
volumes elevated since April and customer satisfaction scores at their lowest recorded level for
the account. The customer's executive sponsor has invoked the escalation provision of the MSA and
has placed the FY2027 renewal "under active review". The Board noted that the notice window for
non-renewal opens in November 2026 and asked management to prepare a retention plan. **ARR at risk
is the full Vantage annual fee.**

**Item 5 — Northwind Retail Group.**
The General Counsel reminded the Board that the Northwind MSA expires on 30 June 2026 with no
automatic renewal, and that **Section 14.2 treats a change of control of the Company as an
assignment requiring Northwind's prior written consent**. Counsel advised that consent should be
sought early in any sale process. The Board noted the account represents the largest single
revenue relationship.

**Item 6 — Pricing Governance.**
The CRO reported that the discount granted to Pinnacle Outdoor in March 2025 was above the
standard approval threshold and was approved on a strategic basis. **The General Counsel asked
that the Vantage most-favoured-customer provision be reviewed in light of this.** Action: CFO and
GC to report back. *(No further reference to this action appears in subsequent minutes provided in
the data room.)*

**Item 7 — Legal.**
The CFO reported that the third annual settlement under the ongoing series of commercial disputes
with former channel partners had been concluded at $2.1M, consistent with the prior two years.
The Board asked whether provision should be made for a further settlement in FY2026. The CFO
responded that the matters are being treated as non-recurring for the purposes of the sale
process.

**Item 9 — European Strategy.**
Management presented the plan for direct European expansion from FY2026. **A director asked
whether the Atlas Distribution arrangement permits direct sales in Europe. Management undertook to
revert.**

**Item 11 — Working Capital.**
The CFO noted that receivables from the three largest customers had extended materially during the
year and that the collections team had been re-prioritised accordingly.
