# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Page 1 — Term.** Initial Term of thirty-six (36) months from 1 January 2023, expiring
31 December 2026.

**Page 3 — Section 3.1 Renewal.** This Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods
of twelve (12) months unless either party gives not less than **sixty (60) days'** written notice
of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current term.

**Page 9 — Section 8.4 Most Favoured Customer Pricing.** Supplier warrants that the effective
discount applied to Customer under this Agreement, being eighteen per cent (18%), **is and shall
remain not less favourable than the effective discount granted by Supplier to any other customer
of comparable annual committed spend.** Where Supplier grants a more favourable effective discount
to any such customer, Supplier shall (a) notify Customer within thirty (30) days, and (b) apply
the more favourable discount to Customer **retrospectively from the date such discount was first
granted to the other customer**, issuing a credit note in respect of the difference.

**Page 10 — Section 8.5.** "Comparable annual committed spend" means annual committed spend of not
less than fifty per cent (50%) of Customer's then-current Annual Subscription Fee.

**Page 16 — Section 12.2 Service Levels.** Availability target of 99.5% measured monthly.
Where availability falls below 99.0% in any two consecutive months, **Customer may terminate for
material breach on thirty (30) days' notice.**

**Page 17 — Section 12.3 Escalation.** Where the number of Severity 1 incidents exceeds four (4)
in any rolling three (3) month period, the matter shall be escalated to the executive sponsors of
both parties and Customer's remedies under Section 12.2 shall be available.

**Page 24 — Section 18.1 Assignment.** This Agreement may be assigned by either party to a
successor in interest without consent, provided the assignee assumes all obligations in writing.
