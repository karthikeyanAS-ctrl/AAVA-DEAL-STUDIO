# Harborline Systems, Inc. — Confidential Information Memorandum (Excerpt)

## Page 9 — Financial Highlights

Harborline has delivered consistent growth across the review period, with **revenue growing at a
14% CAGR over FY2023–FY2025**. The business has scaled from $34.5M in FY2023 to over $42M in FY2025
while expanding margins.

## Page 11 — Revenue Quality

Harborline operates a subscription-first commercial model. **94% of revenue is recurring
subscription revenue**, with professional services deliberately constrained to implementation and
priced at cost as a customer-acquisition investment. Management does not view services as a
material component of the earnings stream.

## Page 12 — Customer Base

The customer base comprises 214 logos across specialty retail, grocery, and food & beverage.
The portfolio is well diversified: **no single customer accounts for more than 10% of revenue**,
and the top ten customers represent a modest share of the total. Diversification has improved
each year of the review period.

## Page 13 — Customer Tenure

**The top 10 customers have an average tenure of 6 years.** Relationships are deeply embedded in
customer operations, with the platform integrated into merchandising, replenishment, and supplier
workflows.

## Page 14 — Retention

Harborline's retention profile is best in class for the segment:

- **Net revenue retention of 118%** demonstrates best-in-class expansion within the installed base
- **Gross revenue retention is 97%**
- **Logo churn has remained below 5% annually** across the review period

Expansion is driven by module attach and seat growth. FY2025 saw a notable expansion at Cascade
Foods Holdings, which materially increased its committed spend during the year.

## Page 18 — Adjusted EBITDA

**Adjusted EBITDA of $12.4M represents a 32% margin.** The adjustments schedule is set out in the
management QoE pack and comprises non-recurring items, owner compensation normalisation, and
identified pro-forma effects.

## Page 22 — Working Capital

The business collects reliably from a blue-chip customer base. **Days sales outstanding is stable
at approximately 45 days** and management does not anticipate a working capital drag post-close.

## Page 27 — Contracts

Contracting is standardised on the Harborline Master Services Agreement. **All customer contracts
are freely assignable on a change of control** and no consent is required from any counterparty in
connection with a sale of the Company. **No contract contains most-favoured-nation pricing.**

## Page 31 — Growth Strategy

**Direct expansion into Europe is the primary growth vector, worth $6M ARR by FY2028.** Harborline
intends to establish a direct sales presence in the UK, Germany, and the Netherlands from FY2026,
building on inbound demand already observed in those markets.
