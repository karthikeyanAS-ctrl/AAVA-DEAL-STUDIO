# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Page 1 — Recitals.** Cascade Foods Holdings LLC ("Customer") is the holding entity of the
Cascade Foods group. **Customer was previously party to a Master Services Agreement with Supplier
dated 1 March 2021 (the "Prior Agreement") in the name of its operating subsidiary Cascade Foods,
which Prior Agreement expired on 28 February 2025 and was not renewed at that time.**

**Page 1 — Recital B.** Following a competitive re-evaluation, Customer has elected to re-engage
Supplier under revised commercial terms set out herein.

**Page 2 — Term.** Thirty-six (36) months from 1 June 2025.

**Page 7 — Section 6.1 Fees.** Discount of twenty-one per cent (21%) against list, an increase
from the fifteen per cent (15%) applied under the Prior Agreement, reflecting the interruption in
service and Customer's re-tendering of the requirement.

**Page 8 — Section 6.3 Onboarding Credit.** Supplier shall grant a one-time credit of $180,000
against implementation fees in recognition of the re-implementation required following the lapse
of the Prior Agreement.

**Page 20 — Section 15.1 Assignment.** Assignable to a successor in interest without consent.
