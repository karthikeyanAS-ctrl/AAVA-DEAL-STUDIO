# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Page 1 — Parties and Term.** This Master Services Agreement is entered into as of 1 July 2022
between Harborline Systems, Inc. ("Supplier") and Northwind Retail Group, Inc. ("Customer").
The Initial Term commences on the Effective Date and expires on **30 June 2026**.

**Page 2 — Section 2.3 Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term this Agreement shall terminate
automatically. **There shall be no automatic renewal.** Any renewal shall be by written agreement
of both parties executed not less than ninety (90) days prior to expiry, and Customer shall be
under no obligation to renew.

**Page 11 — Section 9.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the Annual Subscription Fee set out in the
applicable Order Form, subject to the discount of twelve per cent (12%) agreed at Schedule 3.

**Page 19 — Section 14.2 Assignment and Change of Control.** Neither party may assign this
Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. **For the avoidance of doubt, a
Change of Control of Supplier shall constitute an assignment for the purposes of this Section
14.2.** Supplier shall notify Customer in writing of any proposed Change of Control not less than
thirty (30) days prior to completion.

**Page 19 — Section 14.3.** In the event a Change of Control of Supplier completes without
Customer's prior written consent, **Customer may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days'
written notice** given at any time within one hundred and eighty (180) days of becoming aware of
such Change of Control, without liability and without payment of any termination charge.

**Page 20 — Section 14.4.** Where Customer consents to a Change of Control, Customer may require
a re-negotiation of the Annual Subscription Fee to reflect the revised supplier covenant, such
re-negotiation to be conducted in good faith.

**Page 23 — Section 17.1 Liability Cap.** Supplier's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees
paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

**Page 28 — Section 21.4 Service Credits.** Sustained failure to meet the 99.5% availability
target in any three consecutive months entitles Customer to a service credit of up to 8% of the
Annual Subscription Fee.
