# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Page 1 — Term.** Thirty-six (36) months from 1 April 2025.

**Page 2 — Section 2.2 Renewal.** Automatic renewal for successive twelve (12) month periods,
sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.

**Page 8 — Section 7.1 Fees and Discount.** In consideration of Customer's committed volume and
its agreement to act as a reference account, Supplier grants a discount of **thirty-four per cent
(34%)** against list pricing for the duration of the Initial Term. Customer's Annual Subscription
Fee for Year 1 is set out at Schedule 2.

**Page 8 — Section 7.2.** The discount at Section 7.1 was approved by Supplier's Chief Revenue
Officer on 14 March 2025 as a strategic account concession.

**Page 12 — Section 9.6 Price Escalator.** Fees shall increase annually on each anniversary by the
lesser of (a) the change in CPI-U over the preceding twelve months and (b) three per cent (3%).

**Page 21 — Section 16.1 Assignment.** Assignable to a successor in interest without consent.
