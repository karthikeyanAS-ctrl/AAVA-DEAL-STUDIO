## 2. Investment Thesis

Harborline Systems is a B2B SaaS platform serving the retail and grocery sector with FY2025 revenue of $42.7M [E-001], growing at an independently verified CAGR of 11.3% over FY2023–FY2025 [E-002] — below management's claimed 14.0% [E-034]. The platform has a tenured top-10 customer base averaging 5.6 years [E-006] and closing ARR of $38.7M. These elements of the investment case are supported by verified evidence.

However, diligence has materially challenged the underwriting case across four dimensions. First, the earnings quality is significantly weaker than presented: management's Adjusted EBITDA of $12.4M [E-008] is reduced to a Team Adjusted EBITDA of $7.25M [E-009] after rejecting three adjustments totalling $5.15M — a recurring legal settlement misclassified as non-recurring, a prospective unactioned cost saving, and a pro-forma new logo annualisation. Second, the revenue-quality profile is weaker than claimed: NRR is 100.5% [E-004] vs the claimed 118%, logo churn is 9.4% [E-004] vs the claimed below-5%, and independently restated recurring revenue is 87.4% [E-005] vs the claimed 94%. Third, customer concentration is extreme and undisclosed: the single largest customer (Northwind) represents 27.4% of FY2025 revenue [E-003, E-027] and carries a change-of-control consent requirement and imminent renewal risk. Fourth, the primary growth thesis — direct European expansion worth $6M ARR by FY2028 — is directly blocked by an executed Atlas Distribution exclusivity covenant covering the EU, UK, Switzerland, and Norway through 31 December 2029 [E-047, E-048]. The Cascade Foods re-engagement classified as a new logo win is a management credibility concern [E-049, E-050, E-021]. The EBITDA bridge vs claims-scoreboard inconsistency remains unresolved and must be reconciled before final IC reliance.

## 3. Findings That Change Price or Transaction Terms

### 3.1 CRITICAL — Northwind Change-of-Control Consent and Renewal Risk (RF-001)

**Finding:** The executed Northwind MSA (Section 14.2) treats a change of control of Harborline as an assignment requiring Northwind's prior written consent; if the transaction completes without consent, Northwind may terminate on 90 days' notice within 180 days of awareness (Section 14.3) [E-035, E-036]. The contract expires 30 June 2026 with no automatic renewal; a bilateral written renewal must be executed at least 90 days before expiry, meaning the renewal window opens on or before 31 March 2026 — coinciding with the expected close date [E-037]. The Board's own General Counsel confirmed the restriction in October 2025 board minutes [E-052]. Management's CIM and contract summary both incorrectly state no change-of-control restriction exists.

**Deal Impact:** Northwind represents 27.4% of FY2025 revenue ($11.7M) [E-027] and $10.55M of closing ARR (27.3% of total) [E-017]. ARR-risk EV impact: **($17,719,028)** at deal-team assumption parameters (CM 40%, LP 35%, 12× multiple) — parameters are assumptions, not observed facts. Additional unquantified risk: Section 14.4 permits Northwind to require fee re-negotiation upon consenting to the change of control.

**Evidence:** [E-017, E-035, E-036, E-037, E-052]

**Required Protection:** Consider with transaction counsel: (i) condition precedent to closing requiring Northwind's written consent; (ii) specific indemnity covering losses from Section 14.3 termination; (iii) escrow/holdback sized to Northwind ARR exposure if consent not obtained pre-close; (iv) seller covenant to use best efforts to obtain consent and execute renewal before closing; (v) representation and warranty that no counterparty has indicated intention to exercise termination rights.

### 3.6 HIGH — Atlas Distribution Exclusivity Blocks European Direct Expansion (RF-006)

**Finding:** The CIM's primary growth thesis is direct European expansion worth $6M ARR by FY2028 (CLM-07, Contradicted). The executed Atlas Distribution reseller agreement (Section 4.3) prohibits Harborline from directly or indirectly marketing, licensing, selling, or delivering the platform to any customer in the EU, UK, Switzerland, or Norway through 31 December 2029 [E-047]. The exclusivity survives assignment and binds any successor in interest (Section 22.1) [E-048]. SQL data shows 41 direct customers in the territory, 10 of whom started after the exclusivity effective date (January 2024) [E-022], raising a potential breach question. The board asked in October 2025 whether the Atlas arrangement permits direct European sales, with management undertaking to revert — with no documented resolution. The IT overview confirms no EU data residency capability exists today [E-055].

> **Reviewer Qualification:** Treat contractual interpretation and enforceability as matters for transaction counsel. Use ARR or EV impact only where the corresponding evidence is resolved. The ARR-at-risk figure ($1,830,725) and EV impact use deal-team assumption parameters.

**Deal Impact:** ARR at risk: $1,830,725 (4.7% of closing ARR) [E-023]. ARR-risk EV impact: **($3,075,619)** at assumption parameters. The $6M ARR growth plan is a forecast and cannot be priced as lost ARR under the stated methodology; the growth thesis as presented in the CIM is not achievable through direct sales during the Atlas term without renegotiation.

**Evidence:** [E-022, E-023, E-047, E-048, E-055]

**Required Protection:** Consider with transaction counsel: (i) specific indemnity covering breach-of-exclusivity claims by Atlas from direct territory customers onboarded after January 2024; (ii) condition precedent or closing deliverable requiring Atlas's written confirmation of permitted direct sales scope; (iii) purchase-price adjustment or earn-out reduction if European growth plan cannot be executed as presented; (iv) representation and warranty that no breach of the Atlas exclusivity covenant has occurred.
