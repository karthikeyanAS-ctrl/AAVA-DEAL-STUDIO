## 2. Investment Thesis

Harborline Systems is a B2B SaaS platform serving the retail and grocery sector, generating FY2025 revenue of $42.7M [E-001] at an independently verified CAGR of 11.3% over FY2023–FY2025 [E-002] — below management's claimed 14.0% [E-034]. Diligence supports a recurring-revenue business with a subscription-delivery core; however, independently restated recurring revenue is 87.4% of total [E-005], materially below management's claimed 94.0%, due to $4.7M of Professional Services revenue tagged as recurring but delivered on a one-time basis. Closing ARR is $38.7M [E-017 context], with NRR of 100.5% and GRR of 93.2% at the customer level [E-004] — both materially below management's claimed 118% NRR and 97% GRR. Logo churn of 9.4% [E-004] exceeds management's claimed sub-5% threshold. The top-10 customer average tenure of 5.6 years [E-006] is broadly consistent with management's claim of 6 years, though one top-10 customer (Cascade Foods Holdings) has only 0.84 years of tenure under its current entity.

What diligence supports: A real subscription software business with genuine recurring revenue, a multi-year customer base, and a defensible platform product. The Northwind, Vantage, Pinnacle, and Atlas relationships represent long-standing commercial engagements. The FY2025 EBITDA bridge reconciles at the management level ($4.28M reported + $8.12M adjustments = $12.4M management adjusted) [E-007, E-008].

What diligence challenges: Multiple management representations in the CIM are contradicted by verified evidence — most critically, the change-of-control assignability claim [E-035, E-052], the MFN-free claim [E-041, E-053], the customer concentration claim [E-027], and the NRR/churn metrics [E-004, E-029]. Three EBITDA adjustments totalling $5.25M have been rejected (ADJ-003, ADJ-005) or remain challenged (ADJ-010), reducing the verified earnings base materially. The primary growth thesis — direct European expansion to $6M ARR by FY2028 — is directly blocked by the Atlas exclusivity covenant through 31 December 2029 [E-047].

What remains unresolved: The EBITDA bridge vs. claims scoreboard discrepancy ($7.25M vs. $5.46M Team Adjusted EBITDA); the ADJ-007 discontinued product adjustment ($540K, challenged); the ADJ-010 new logo annualisation ($1.65M, challenged); the Vantage MFN credit quantum; the Atlas breach exposure for 10 post-exclusivity direct territory customers; and the FY2026 legal settlement exposure.

The contract expires 30 June 2026 with no automatic renewal; a bilateral written renewal must be executed at least 90 days before expiry, meaning the renewal window opens on or before 31 March 2026 — coinciding with the expected close date [E-037]. The Board's own General Counsel confirmed the consent requirement in October 2025 board minutes [E-052], directly contradicting the CIM's representation that all contracts are freely assignable [E-035].
